Prop settings

These settings stay on this device. They never appear on camera.

Add an English translation after a bar, for example 0:12 Aissera… | Last night…. Rows without a timestamp follow the previous row by 4 seconds. A row with only ♪ shows a music break. The player starts at 0:00.

Operator controls

  • Reset: tap anywhere along the top of the screen (it flashes faintly), or press R / Esc. Works on every screen after the start screen.
  • Start listening: tap the orb, or press Space.
  • Open the lyrics: tap Play full song on the song-found screen, or press Space. The screen waits there until then.
  • Pause / play lyrics: tap the play button, or press Space. Tap or drag the progress bar to jump.
  • Jump to a line: tap it. Step lyrics: → / ←.
  • Open these settings: press and hold the top-right corner of the start screen, or press S.
