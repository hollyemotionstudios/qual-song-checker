Prop settings

These settings stay on this device. They never appear on camera.

Rows without a timestamp follow the previous row by 4 seconds. A row with only ♪ shows a music break. The song clock starts 3 seconds before the first row.

Operator controls

  • Reset: tap anywhere along the top of the screen (it flashes faintly), or press R / Esc. Works on every screen after the start screen.
  • Start listening: tap the orb, or press Space.
  • Skip to lyrics: tap the song-found screen.
  • Step lyrics: → / ←, or tap the lyrics in tap mode.
  • Open these settings: press and hold the top-right corner of the start screen, or press S.
